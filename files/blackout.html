Blackout

Share the document, not the details.

Upload a document, scan or image. Blackout finds personal details on your device, covers them, and gives the file back in the same format.

  • FindsNames, phone numbers, emails, IDs and addresses in text files, Word, PDFs, scans and images.
  • CoversYou choose what to hide, review a preview, and cover anything it missed.
  • Stays privateEverything runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or sent to an AI service.

Choose what to hide

Types you leave unselected stay visible, and you can switch them on later in the preview. Face is the exception: it is only searched for when selected. Faces in images and scans are blurred. Signatures and handwriting are not detected, so draw over them in the preview.

No file handy?

Your files stay on your device. Nothing is uploaded or sent to an AI service. The page downloads its helper libraries (PDF reading, text recognition, name detection) from public CDNs the first time they are needed, and everything else runs in your browser. Review the result before sharing it.

Detection test (advanced)

Run the detection rules against a labelled test file from this device and see precision, recall and F1. Nothing is uploaded, stored or logged. Test file format: one JSON object per line with id, text and spans (start, end, label).
